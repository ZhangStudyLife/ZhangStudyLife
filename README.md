## Featured Competition Project

### [Fly Over Minefield · 飞跃雷区](https://github.com/ZhangStudyLife/HDUASC-SmartCar-21st-FlyOverMinefield)

**2026 National Champion · 第 21 届全国大学生智能汽车竞赛「飞跃雷区」组**  
Hangzhou Dianzi University · 飞跃雷区三队

An air–ground collaborative system: a drone identifies beacons and vehicle lights, the flight controller fuses visual and flight-state information and plans the route, and the ground vehicle executes velocity commands.

- **Flight control:** dual-core MCU firmware, IMU attitude estimation, altitude estimation and control.
- **Perception to motion:** camera modeling, three-camera fusion, CarPlan3 path planning and air–ground communication.
- **Open engineering:** source code, PCB designs, mechanical files, debugging tools and documentation.

[Explore the full project](https://github.com/ZhangStudyLife/HDUASC-SmartCar-21st-FlyOverMinefield) · [Air flight controller](https://github.com/ZhangStudyLife/CYT4BB7_Air) · [Competition run](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1hkh36wECt/)
